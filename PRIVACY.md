# Open Receipt Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Open Receipt does not collect your data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking, and the app has no server of its own.

## Data on your device

Receipts you scan, import, or create are stored only on your device, together with their page images, items, and splits. The same applies to the people you add and their payment details. Your device backups include this data in the same way as other app data. Deleted receipts stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and then the app removes them. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

## Receipt reading

When you scan or import a receipt, Open Receipt sends the page images to Apple Intelligence to read the items and totals. Apple processes the images with [Private Cloud Compute](https://security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/), which does not keep them after the request, and the developer never receives them. [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers this processing.

## Contacts

Open Receipt asks for Contacts access only when you add people to a receipt or choose your own contact. It reads names, phone numbers, email addresses, and photos on your device, and you can grant access to selected contacts only. The app saves the details of the people you add to receipts on your device. It never uploads your contacts.

## Payment requests

When you send a payment request, Open Receipt opens Venmo, Cash App, or Messages. It fills in the recipient, amount, and note, and nothing is sent until you confirm in that app. The privacy policy of the app you use then applies.
